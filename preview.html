Orange County, California

A local expression of Search Ministries.

For nearly 50 years Search Ministries has helped adults explore questions about God in safe, relational settings. Search OC has served Orange County for over 40 years.

Our mission is simple: create spaces where people can be curious about God without feeling judged or pressured.

Our Approach
  • 1 Value the one in front of you.
  • 2 Trust God with the results. Spiritual growth is a process.
  • 3 Address real barriers with gentleness and honesty.

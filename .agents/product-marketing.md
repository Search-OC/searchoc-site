## Product Overview

**One-liner:** Search OC hosts Open Forums in Orange County, a table where people ask life's big
questions out loud.

**What it does:** An Open Forum is a hosted, one-hour conversation over food and drinks, usually 15 to twenty people. A moderator keeps it honest and guests speak first. Around that table sit two supporting programs: Formation, a twelve-week cohort that trains Christians to have these conversations without guilt or pressure, and Foundations, a book series for people who want to keep going after the questions start landing.

**Product category:** Local ministry hosting open conversations about faith and meaning. Not a
church, not a class, not an apologetics debate series.

**Product type:** In-person gatherings plus supporting curriculum. Free to attend.

**Business model:** Nonprofit. A local expression of Search Ministries. Nothing on the site is sold,
so there is no pricing, no trial, and no purchase to optimize.

## Target Audience

The site serves two audiences that must not be blended into one voice.

**Audience A, the person with the question.** Someone who walked away from church, or who never found a group that goes that deep, and still wants to think critically about what they are experiencing. They are the reason `/` exists. They often arrive by searching Search OC after meeting someone from the ministry, so the homepage has to work as a standalone answer to "what is this." Do not publish personal names on the public site; contact is a mailto link only.

**Audience B, the Christian who wants to talk about faith without being weird about it.** They feel
guilty about evangelism, have tried scripts that did not fit, and want a way that matches real
relationships. They are the reason `/formation`, `/foundation`, and `/resources` exist.

**Primary use case:** Get someone to one table, one time, for one honest hour.

**Jobs to be done:**

- For Audience A: ask the question I have been carrying without being recruited or corrected.
- For Audience B: invite a friend into a conversation about faith without damaging the friendship.
- For Audience B: keep growing after the first conversation instead of stalling out.

## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem:** There are no safe rooms for the big questions that are open and without an agenda. People carrying real doubt have nowhere to say it out loud where they will not be argued with, recruited, or made into a project.

**Why alternatives fall short:**

- Church services are one-directional, so there is no place to push back.
- Small groups usually assume belief before you walk in.
- Online debate rewards winning rather than understanding.
- Apologetics events treat the question as something to defeat rather than sit with.

**What it costs them:** Years of carrying a question alone, and often the loss of a community they
once had.

**Emotional tension:** For Audience A, the fear of being handled. For Audience B, guilt about not
sharing faith combined with the fear of making things awkward with someone they love.

## Competitive Landscape

Reframed as alternatives, since nothing here is competitive in a commercial sense.

**Direct:** Other faith discussion groups, Alpha courses. These usually run on a fixed curriculum and
assume attendees will progress through it, which raises the cost of showing up once.

**Secondary:** Church small groups. They tend to presuppose shared belief, so a genuine skeptic
arrives already out of step with the room.

**Indirect:** Doing nothing, or working through the question privately with podcasts and books. Low
risk and genuinely useful, but with no one to answer back.

## Differentiation

**Key differentiators:**

- Coming once is a complete and acceptable outcome. Nothing is designed to escalate.
- Guests speak first, and listening the entire time is allowed.
- No photography and no social posting about who attended.
- Forty years of doing it locally, so this is not a new format being tested on people.

**How we do it differently:** The table is the product. Food, drinks, one hour, one question, then
people go home.

**Why that is better:** It lowers the cost of attending to almost nothing, which is the only way a
genuinely skeptical person says yes.

## Objections


| Objection                                            | Response                                                                       |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Am I going to get preached at or recruited?          | No lecture and no ask at the end. Guests speak first, and coming once is fine. |
| Do I have to believe something to come?              | No. The room is built for people who are exploring or unconvinced.             |
| Will I be put on the spot?                           | You can listen the whole time. Participation is always optional.               |
| Will people know I was there?                        | No photos, no video, no social posting about who attends.                      |
| What if my friend asks me something I cannot answer? | Say "I do not know, come ask it out loud." That is the entire job.             |


**Anti-persona:** Someone looking for a debate to win, or a Christian wanting a venue to deliver a
prepared pitch. Both break the room for everyone else.

## Switching Dynamics

**Push:** A question that will not go away, or guilt about never talking about faith.
**Pull:** A table where the question is welcome and nothing is expected afterward.
**Habit:** Silence. Not raising it has worked well enough for years.
**Anxiety:** Being made into someone's project, or damaging a friendship by bringing it up.

## Brand Voice

**Tone:** Calm, plain, unhurried. It reassures by subtraction, naming what will not happen.

**Style:** Short declarative sentences. Second person. Concrete nouns over abstractions, so a table
rather than a community, an hour rather than a commitment. Body copy avoids contractions and writes
"do not" and "is not" in full; quoted text messages and FAQ answers may relax this. Negation is a
core device: "No lecture. No pressure." Sentences frequently close on a flat, final beat, as in
"That is the whole ask."

**Personality:** Honest, unhurried, self-aware, hospitable, specific.

## Site Architecture Note

The homepage is deliberately isolated and links to no inner page, so a skeptic who searches Search OC
lands on the mission and the capture form without being routed into material written for
Christians. The inner pages, `/formation`, `/foundation`, `/resources`, and `/invite`, cross-link
freely and route back to `/open-forums`, which carries the same mission content plus its FAQ and
forms. Copy written for any inner page may assume more context than copy written for `/`.
